# 58 Joralemon Street — source notes (facts used for the picture)

Research 2026-11-12 (web search; Wikipedia and Brownstoner pages could not be fetched from this environment, so the facts
below come from search-result summaries of those pages and of Untapped New York, Fast Company, Inhabitat, Refinery29).

| fact | how it is used |
|---|---|
| Greek Revival row house, built **1847** as a private residence, Brooklyn Heights (Joralemon St near Willow St) | 1847-style family parlour in scene 7; neighbours are row houses of the same age |
| **Three storeys** (over a raised basement), red brick | 3 storeys + basement, red brick (CC0 brick scan), cornice |
| Bought and gutted by the **Interborough Rapid Transit Co. (IRT)** c. 1907; became a ventilator + **emergency exit** for the east end of the **Joralemon Street Tunnel** (IRT Lexington Ave line; first train Nov 1907, opened 9 Jan 1908) | scenes 8–11 set in the 1900s: tunnel works, engineers, conversion |
| Board of Transportation 1940, NYC Transit Authority 1953 (current owner); in the Brooklyn Heights Historic District (1965) | not shown (no text on screen) |
| Today: windows, muntins, frames **and lintels all jet black**, permanently shut (Lexan since 1999; louvres before) | modern-era facade: black window units + black lintels; the historic era has normal painted sashes, curtains, lit rooms |
| **Thick metal door with a grate** (neighbours have decorated wood-and-glass doors); front **stoop** with **iron railings**; door marked **58** | black steel door with a small grille, brass "58" (the only in-world text), stoop + railings |

Not verified (modelled as typical for an 1847 Brooklyn Heights Greek Revival row house): three window bays, entrance in
the left bay up the stoop, brownstone sills, dentilled wooden cornice, areaway with an iron fence, basement windows with
grilles, ~6.4 m (21 ft) frontage. The interior machinery, shaft and tunnel geometry are ILLUSTRATIVE (a cutaway
diagram), not the real equipment layout; the emergency exit is shown only as a generic stair + door.
