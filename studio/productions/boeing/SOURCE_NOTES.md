# Source notes (checked 2026-11-21)

Sources: 99% Invisible, "Prop Town"; Cascade PBS, Mossback's Northwest; Warfare History Network (Bill Yenne); Flying magazine, "Remembering Boeing's Plant 2".

## Documented facts used in the film
- Boeing **Plant 2** stood on the Duwamish waterway in Seattle. It was the main B-17 Flying Fortress plant; 6,981 B-17s were produced there.
- During WWII the plant was camouflaged against air attack by a fake residential neighbourhood on its roof, built around 1944 and removed around 1946.
- **Materials:**
  - houses made largely of canvas over light timber frames;
  - shrubs of burlap over chicken-wire frames;
  - trees of chicken wire with feathers glued on;
  - painted "streets", some signposted with joke names such as **Burlap Boulevard** and **Synthetic Street**.
- It was designed with the help of a Hollywood set designer.

## Deliberately NOT stated
- The designer's name: sources disagree (John Stewart Detlie vs G. W. Dennis).
- Exact house counts and exact building dimensions.
- The film's factory (220 × 130 m) is a scaled, simplified stand-in; the real plant was larger.
