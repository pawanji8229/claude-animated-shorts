# TwistFrame Lab #8: "The Fake Neighborhood Above Boeing's Factory" (storyboard)

**Format:** 1550 frames at 30 fps (51.644 s, confirmed with ffprobe). Native 1080×1920. The narration is the master clock.

**Transcript:** the ASR mis-hears a few words. This is the planning reading, consistent with the timing:
> They built an entire fake neighborhood on top of an airplane factory. And from above, it was supposed to look like a normal town. You see, during World War Two, Boeing had an enormous factory in Seattle. Inside, the workers were building military aircraft. But officials were concerned that the factory might be spotted during aerial surveillance. And the building was far too large to simply hide behind a few trees. So they came up with a ridiculous-looking solution. They built a neighborhood on the roof. Workers constructed fake houses, roads, gardens and trees. From the ground, the whole thing looked like an unusual collection of props. But from high in the sky, the factory was supposed to blend into the surrounding neighborhoods. Underneath the fake town, the real factory continued operating. And that explains why…

**Two worlds, one geography:** the factory (220 × 130 m, roof at 16 m) stands at the origin by the Duwamish River. Its roof carries the fake town, and real neighbourhoods surround it. Layers are joined by opacity dissolves; the last second dissolves into the opening shot, so the video loops.
