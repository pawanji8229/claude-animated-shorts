# Source notes — "reopening"
- The rebuilt London Bridge at Lake Havasu City, Arizona, was formally dedicated on 10 October 1971 with a large public
  celebration (parade, fireworks, hot-air balloons were part of the period festivities; exact programme varies by source).
- Water under the bridge comes from the Bridgewater Channel, dredged beneath the bridge so it spans a waterway.
- Visual liberties (stylised, not documentary): generic pennant bunting, small motorboats, an original businessman
  character (no likeness of Robert P. McCulloch), confetti and flash bulbs to visualise "the world's attention".
