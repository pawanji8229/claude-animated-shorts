**Format:** ONE continuous camera, 9:16, seamless loop. No captions; the only text is in-world signage (hotel sign, a "2019" desk calendar, room number).

## Geography: one connected building

- **Exterior** (z > 0): street and pavement, a glass entrance with automatic sliding doors, and a hotel sign.
- **Lobby** (x −9..9, z −14..0):
  - reception desk at the back, with the **raptor receptionist** and an **android receptionist**;
  - lounge on the left (Poly Haven sofas, chairs, tables, plants);
  - the **robot workforce** in charging alcoves on the right wall;
  - a concierge robot by the corridor mouth, a floor-cleaning robot, and the staff office door.
- **Corridor** (x 9..24): carpet, room doors.
- **Room 101** (x 17..23, north side): bed, nightstand with the **bedside egg robot**, window.

## Sound

Every cue comes from the picture:
- the doors, servo whirs, the raptor's growl and jaw clack, the key card;
- luggage robot wheels and the error beep;
- concierge chirps and the loading tone;
- snores (synthesised breathing), the egg robot's listening tone, its wake-up chime;
- footsteps on marble and carpet, cloth, dolly wheels, power-down tones;
- lobby ambience and soft music.

All levels are set relative to the narration, which is never edited.
