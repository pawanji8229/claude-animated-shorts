# TwistFrame Lab #7: "The Light Bulb That Has Been Glowing Since 1901" (storyboard)

**Format:** 1607 frames at 30 fps (53.551 s), native 1080×1920. The narration is the master clock.
**Script:** the user's wording, confirmed against the ASR timing (ASR ⚠ lines are mis-hearings of that script).

**Structure:** story layers, each with its own camera move and actors, joined by planned opacity dissolves (no hard cuts).
- In era sub-layers (B, E, F) the camera path, the bulb and the hero are IDENTICAL, so only the world changes. The anchor never ghosts.
- The last second dissolves into layer A's pre-roll, so frame 1606 flows into frame 0.
