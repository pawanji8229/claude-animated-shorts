# The World's Shortest Scheduled Flight — source notes

## Facts used (and how the film treats them)
- **Route:** scheduled air service between Westray (airport code WRY, at Aikerness) and Papa Westray (PPW) in the
  Orkney Islands, Scotland. The airfields are about 2.7 km (1.7 miles) apart, across Papa Sound.
- **Flight time:** scheduled as roughly one and a half to two minutes. Airborne time depends on wind:
  - Reported fastest airborne time is about 53 s.
  - Some reports cite 47 s with an ideal wind.
  - The narration's "ninety seconds" is treated as a typical figure. Nothing on screen claims an exact time, and the
    film shows no clock or number.
- **Aircraft:** Britten-Norman BN-2 Islander.
  - Small twin piston-engine (two-blade propellers) high-wing aircraft.
  - Fixed tricycle landing gear: twin-wheel main legs on long faired struts below the engine nacelles, and a single
    steerable nose wheel.
  - Boxy flat-sided fuselage: about 10.9 m long, 14.9 m span, about 4.2 m high.
  - Bench seats with no centre aisle; passengers board through side doors (two to port, one to starboard).
  - Production Islanders have a cantilever wing (no wing struts). The film follows the real aircraft: the visible
    struts are the main-gear legs, and the wing is unbraced.
- **Airfields:**
  - Small island aerodromes with graded or grass runways, a small single-storey terminal building, a fire-cover
    building, a windsock, and boundary fences.
  - No jet bridges and no large terminals.
- **Landscape:**
  - Low green islands of improved pasture and silage fields, separated by stone dykes and wire fences.
  - Single-track roads; white-harled farmhouses with grey slate roofs.
  - Rocky flagstone shores with small sandy bays.
  - Seabirds (gulls, terns, fulmars) and grey North Atlantic water with whitecaps and shore foam.

## Geography (approximate; drawn from general map knowledge, simplified)
- **Westray:**
  - Large island; its northern lobe ends at the Aikerness peninsula, where the airfield sits.
  - Pierowall village and bay lie to the south-west, opening onto Papa Sound.
- **Papa Westray:** long narrow island running north–south, about 6 km by 1–1.5 km, east of Papa Sound.
  - Its airfield is near the middle of the west side.
  - The Holm of Papay islet lies off its east coast.
- **Model coordinates:** metres, origin at the Westray runway, +x east, +z south. The Papa Westray runway is about
  2.75 km east.
- Coastlines are hand-traced approximations, not survey data.
