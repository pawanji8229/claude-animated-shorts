## World

**Coordinates:** metres, +x east, +z south, origin at the Westray runway centre. All story time is 30 fps frames.

**Places:**
- **Westray airfield (WRY):**
  - Gravel/grass runway, E–W, x −300…+300.
  - Small harled terminal and a fire shed on the north apron, windsock, boundary fence.
  - Sea cliffs and rock shelf about 60 m beyond the east end.
- **Papa Westray airfield (PPW):** grass runway, E–W, x ≈ 2.45–2.95 km; hut terminal, windsock, fences, sheep.
- **Papa Sound:** about 1.6 km of open water between the two islands; whitecaps, shore foam on the rock shelves.
- **Wind:** steady breeze from the east-north-east (windsocks stream west); take-off and landing toward the east, into wind.

**Aircraft:** BN-2 Islander, local axes +x nose, +y up, +z starboard.
- **Seating:** row 1 pilot (port), rows 2–5 two-seat benches. The hero sits in row 3 on the starboard window.
  Passenger B ("the side-eye") sits beside him on the port side. Passenger C (calm window-gazer) sits in row 4,
  starboard.
- **Doors:** pilot door at port front, one starboard door, port rear door (used for boarding).
- **Equipment:** two-blade props, twin-wheel main gear on nacelle legs, single nose wheel, cantilever wing.

## Sound plan

All effects are synthesised and come from on-screen actions:
- **Aircraft:** prop drone (exterior/interior variants), start crank → catch → run-up, taxi rumble, take-off power,
  airflow, touchdown chirp, brakes, spin-down.
- **Passengers:** seat creak, cloth, bag, lap-belt clicks, door.
- **Footsteps:** on gravel and grass, from gait contacts.
- **Outdoors:** gulls and terns, wind, grass, windsock flap, sea wash.
- **Mix:** engines and wind duck under the narration, at least 15 dB clear on every word.
- **Loop:** no fade-out; the beds are loop-safe.
